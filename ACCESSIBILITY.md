# Accessibility

EllowDigital aims to make digital experiences usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technology or alternative input methods.

## Our approach

For applicable interfaces, we aim to:

- Use semantic structure, meaningful headings, labels and accessible names.
- Support keyboard navigation and make focus states visible.
- Maintain readable contrast, scalable text and responsive layouts.
- Provide useful form labels and clear error messages.
- Add appropriate alternative text to informative images and avoid relying on colour alone.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences where motion is used.
- Check important journeys with keyboard-only navigation and available accessibility tools.

These are engineering goals, not a claim that every project is fully conformant with a particular standard. Requirements and testing depth depend on the project, platform and applicable obligations.

## Report an accessibility barrier

Open an issue in the relevant repository when it is safe to describe the problem publicly, or email ellowdigitalindia@gmail.com for a private or client-specific concern.

Helpful details include the page or workflow, steps to reproduce, expected and actual behaviour, browser or device, and assistive technology used if relevant. Avoid including personal, health or other sensitive information.

We welcome practical suggestions that make a workflow clearer and more inclusive.
