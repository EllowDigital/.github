# Community Code of Conduct

EllowDigital aims to maintain a respectful, inclusive and constructive environment for everyone who participates in our projects.

## Expected behaviour

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful, professional and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and technical decisions in good faith.
- Give useful, specific feedback without personal attacks.
- Welcome different backgrounds, experience levels and perspectives.
- Respect project boundaries, privacy, licenses and confidentiality.
- Focus discussions on the work, its evidence and its impact.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory or degrading language, threats, intimidation, deliberate disruption, doxxing, sexual harassment, personal attacks, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable. Repeated bad-faith conduct or misuse of project infrastructure may also lead to restrictions.

## Maintainer response

Project maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, restrict participation or take other reasonable steps in response to conduct that violates this policy. Decisions should be proportionate to the behaviour and its impact.

## Reporting a concern

For a conduct concern, contact ellowdigitalindia@gmail.com with the subject **Community conduct concern**. Include the relevant project and links or context where safe. Please do not post private information in a public issue.

Reports will be handled thoughtfully and shared only as needed to assess and address the concern, subject to applicable obligations and safety needs.

## Scope

This policy applies in EllowDigital project spaces and when an individual is acting as a representative of a project or its community. A repository may include additional conduct or moderation rules.

## A good-faith community

We want project spaces to remain safe, useful and focused on learning, building and solving problems together.
